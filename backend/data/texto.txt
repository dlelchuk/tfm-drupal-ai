Policies and guidelines  2.   Didactic  3.   Technology (ICT)  4.   Diversity  5.   Accessibility  These recommendations are intended to guide the teacher in the pro-  cess of   developing or updating an OER specifically in each particular  dimension, thus ensuring its quality.   Each recommendation has a set  of questions associated as a checklist for assessing the product. They  are based on the previous bibliography, citing the source.  Dimensions and sub - dimensions of our framework follow the pattern  of the most of the previous frameworks and guidelines in the litera-  ture, but complete them, specifically the dimensions Technology Di-  versity and Accessibility.
